CORE COMPETENCIES

Our Three Primary Focus Areas

Liberty Technology provides targeted electronics and custom cabling engineered specifically for industrial equipment and vehicle applications.

1. 12V System Controller

Dedicated 12V DC vehicle control units designed for utility trucks, agricultural machines, and auxiliary electrical systems requiring reliable power distribution and digital/analog signal processing.

2. 12V/24V System Controller

Multi-voltage industrial controllers supporting seamless auto-sensing or dual-voltage operation (12V/24V) for heavy equipment, lifts, material handling, and dual-battery vehicle platforms.

3. Remote Controls

Industrial Sub-1GHz wireless key fob transmitters, handheld remotes, and paired receiving units engineered for secure operation of winches, tailgates, hydraulics, and vehicle accessories.

Headquartered in Taipei, Taiwan, Liberty Technology Co., Ltd. (利寶實業) is an engineering company specializing in low-voltage DC control electronics, wireless remote controls, motor controllers, and specialized wire harnesses for industrial vehicle applications.
